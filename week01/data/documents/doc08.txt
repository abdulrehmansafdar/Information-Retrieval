Computer Vision

Computer vision is the part of artificial intelligence that gives a computer the ability to interpret a picture. A camera produces an image, and an algorithm examines its pixels in order to decide what the camera is looking at. Three frequent tasks are image classification, where the whole image receives one label, object detection, where every object is located with a box, and segmentation, where each pixel is assigned to a class so that the shape of an object is described exactly. Training data for these tasks is collected and labelled by human workers, which makes data preparation expensive. Applications include quality control in industry, traffic monitoring, remote sensing and support for medical imaging. A modern vision model is normally a neural network. Such a system must also work in poor light and bad weather, and it should report its uncertainty instead of hiding a wrong detection.
