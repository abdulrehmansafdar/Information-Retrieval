Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence

Cloud computing gives an organisation access to computer resources over a network instead of buying its own hardware. Modern artificial intelligence services are usually delivered in this way, because learning a large model needs far more memory than an ordinary computer can provide. A university or a small company can rent graphics processors for a few hours, train a model and store the result without maintaining a server room. Cloud platforms also offer ready software libraries for data preparation, and they handle security and backup for the user. The advantages are low cost and easy scaling. The disadvantages are a continuing fee, slower response when the connection is poor, and concern about storing sensitive data outside the institution. For that reason a careful organisation keeps a local copy of its data and checks the terms of the service before an experiment becomes part of a real application.
