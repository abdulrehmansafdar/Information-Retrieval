The Future of Artificial Intelligence

The future of artificial intelligence is likely to bring one general system that performs many tasks instead of a collection of narrow applications. Research in this direction aims at software that understands a goal, collects its own information and produces a reliable answer while keeping the data and the computation inside a safe system. Progress will depend less on a single clever algorithm than on a large digital knowledge base and on the computer hardware that can process it. Society will shape the result as well. Regulation, employment in repetitive occupations and public trust in automatic decision making all affect how quickly a new application is accepted. Many experts expect that a doctor, a teacher or an engineer will keep the final responsibility, while the machine supplies the prediction and the options. Understanding these limits is as important as improving accuracy.
