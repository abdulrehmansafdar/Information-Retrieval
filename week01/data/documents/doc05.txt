Artificial Intelligence in Education

Educational software has begun to use artificial intelligence to support teachers and learners in ordinary classroom work. A learning application may examine the answers of a student, detect a repeated mistake and then suggest extra exercises at a suitable level. This adaptive behaviour is possible because the system stores digital information about each learner's progress inside a model. Teachers gain time for personal attention, while students receive feedback immediately instead of waiting for the next lesson. Automatic marking of written work and speech recognition for pronunciation training are further examples of useful automation. Institutions must also think about fairness, because a model trained on historic data may reproduce the bias that already exists in the education system. For that reason a school needs clear rules about what the software may decide and what must be checked by a person.
