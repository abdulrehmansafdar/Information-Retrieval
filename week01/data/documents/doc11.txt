Natural Language Processing in Education

Applying NLP in education means using language technology to support the daily work of teachers and students. Feedback on a written assignment can be produced automatically, and a reading application can ask a simple question about the text of a lesson. Tokenization is the first technical step in such a system, because software must split the learner's text into tokens before it can measure how often important words appear. A language model then compares this information with the material of the course. The result may show that a learner rarely uses connecting words, or that a sentence is difficult to understand. Because education depends on careful reading, teachers should always review such output before a student sees it. Schools also need to protect the digital information stored about each learner, and they must keep the software transparent enough to be trusted.
