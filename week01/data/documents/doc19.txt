Artificial Intelligence and the Internet of Things

The Internet of Things, usually written IoT, describes a large number of small connected devices that sense and transmit data. A sensor in a home, a vehicle or a field sends a short message to a nearby computer, and the information is often stored in a cloud service. Artificial intelligence is added at two points. First, the device itself may use a small model to recognise an event such as a sound or a movement without sending every record away. Second, the collected data is analysed centrally to predict maintenance, to improve energy use or to raise an alarm. IoT networks create real security problems, because thousands of inexpensive devices are often left without software updates. An attacker who enters one weak device may reach the rest of the network. Good practice therefore includes simple passwords, regular updates and automatic removal of devices that no longer answer.
