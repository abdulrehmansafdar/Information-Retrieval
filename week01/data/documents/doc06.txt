Machine Learning in Finance

Finance is an industry in which machine learning has become a normal part of daily computer work. Banks use prediction models to estimate the risk of a loan before a decision is approved, and trading software analyses market data in order to react faster than a human trader. Fraud detection is another important application. An anomaly in the pattern of transactions can be flagged automatically, and a security team then reviews the case. Because money is involved, a model must be explainable and its behaviour must be tested again whenever the market changes. A good algorithm alone is not enough; careful software engineering, such as continuous testing and clear documentation, is essential. Data scientists spend much of their time cleaning data and combining several information sources. Since a wrong prediction is costly, finance rarely allows an automatic system to act without human supervision.
