Deep Learning

Deep learning is an approach to machine learning that uses neural networks with many connected layers. Instead of asking a person to describe important features, the network learns its own representation from the training data. More layers usually mean that the model can recognise more abstract patterns, which is why deep learning performs well on images, speech and language. Training is expensive because a large digital data set must be processed many times, and the network requires computer hardware with a graphics processor. Modern designs include the transformer, which relies on attention instead of processing a text sequence one word at a time. Attention based systems are used today in translation, in search and in language software. The result is a flexible method, but a deep model is difficult to interpret, and its prediction can change after a small change in the input data.
