Autonomous Robots

An autonomous robot is a machine that performs a task without direct human control. It builds an internal map of its environment from sensor data, plans a route and then follows that plan, correcting itself when an unexpected obstacle appears. Navigation in an unknown building or on a public road therefore combines robotics, artificial intelligence and a reliable control system. The software must also decide what to do when a situation is not covered by any training example, which is a research problem in itself. Autonomous vehicles are the most visible application, but the same information is used in agriculture, in warehouses and in search and rescue after a disaster. Legal questions matter as well. Software that drives a vehicle cannot be tested in every case, so regulation is still developing while the technology improves quickly.
