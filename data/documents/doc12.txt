Robotics

Robotics is the engineering field that builds machines able to sense their surroundings and act in the physical world. A robot usually contains a computer, several sensors, motors and a control system that connects them. A camera or a distance sensor produces data about the environment, and software decides how the motors should move. This combination of hardware and software makes robotics different from pure information technology, because the result is visible in the real world. Industrial robots already perform repetitive tasks such as welding and packing with high precision, and the same ideas are used in agriculture and in laboratories. Researchers study how to make a machine grasp an unfamiliar object, because the shape of an object is rarely exactly as expected. Safety remains a major concern, and an automated system must be able to stop before it causes harm.
