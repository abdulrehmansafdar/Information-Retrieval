Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging

Medical imaging is a natural home for computer vision, because a scan is simply a picture with medical meaning. A neural network can be trained on thousands of labelled scans so that it learns what a healthy organ looks like. A segmentation model first outlines the organ and then measures it, while a classification model decides whether the tissue is normal or suspicious. Early results are read by radiologists, who then confirm or reject the suggestion of the system. The benefit is speed and consistency, especially when a hospital must examine many images every day. Careful testing is required, because a scanner of a different type can change the appearance of the image and reduce accuracy. Health authorities therefore ask for validation on local data before a tool is used in real healthcare, and the technology supports professional diagnosis rather than replacing it.
