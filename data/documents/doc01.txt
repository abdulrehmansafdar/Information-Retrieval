Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is a branch of computer science that builds software able to perform tasks which normally require human intelligence. Such systems learn from data instead of following rigid instructions written by a programmer. Modern artificial intelligence systems rely on large collections of digital information, statistical models and algorithms that identify patterns inside that information. A model is trained on this data and is then able to make a prediction about a new case. Researchers divide the field into narrow areas such as machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing and robotics. Companies use these technologies to build applications for medicine, education, finance and industry. The practical goal is not to imitate a human being perfectly, but to support one clearly defined task reliably and quickly. Artificial intelligence also raises questions about transparency, privacy and the effect of automation on employment.
