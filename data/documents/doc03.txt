Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Healthcare is one of the fastest growing areas of artificial intelligence application. Hospitals and clinics collect large amounts of medical data, including laboratory results, patient records and digital images, and machine learning methods are used to search this information for useful patterns. An intelligent system can support diagnosis by comparing the data of one patient with thousands of earlier cases and by estimating the probability of a disease. Doctors do not lose their role; the software provides a second opinion and helps to reduce human error. Prediction models are also used to plan treatment, to manage hospital beds and to monitor patients at home through connected sensors. The main obstacles are privacy, data quality and the need for clear rules about responsibility for a wrong recommendation. Health agencies therefore require validation before any new tool is used in real clinical practice.
